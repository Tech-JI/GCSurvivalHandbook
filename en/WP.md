## **International Winter Program** (WP)

Every year from January to February, students participating in the **Winter Program** (**WP**) travel to four continents for 4-6 weeks to study culture, business, engineering, or language courses. Each program is uniquely tailored to its destination's cultural characteristics and includes a period of free travel, giving participants the chance to independently explore their host country.

In 2025, students visited 11 countries and regions including the UK, Germany, France, Spain, Hungary, Croatia, Japan, South Korea, New Zealand, and Argentina.

**For details, refer to:**

1. **GC Official Website**: [exchange-programs](https://gc.sjtu.edu.cn/cn/academics/ipo/study-abroad/exchange-programs)

2. **WeChat Official Account**: **GC看世界** (GC See the World), featuring detailed lifestyle content and photos created by participating students.

**Reminders:**

1. Interested students should prepare their **passport** well in advance
2. Check the application requirements carefully — some programs are only open to **upper-year** students
3. Pay attention to the type and number of credits each program offers
4. **WP** credits **cannot be double-counted** across **UM** and **SJTU**
5. **WP coursework is not a walk in the park!** It is possible to fail and not receive credits, so you still need to put in the work

## **Domestic Winter Program** (DWP)

In addition to the overseas **WP**, the domestic winter program is called **DWP** (**Domestic Winter Program**).

The 2025 winter programs included destinations in Guizhou, Chang'an, Yunnan, and others. Faculty members such as Dr. Manuel, Dr. Ryan, and Dr. Xu Tong led the groups.

**Reminders:**

1. **DWP** course fees are charged at the **spring semester credit rate**; refer to the **2025 Spring Course List** for specifics.
