# **Student Organizations**

The college boasts a diverse and high-caliber **student organization system** that provides students with a comprehensive platform for growth. Here, you'll not only meet like-minded peers but also cultivate leadership, professional skills, and team spirit. Joining a **student organization** means gaining valuable hands-on experience, lasting friendships, unique campus memories, and the core competencies that give you an edge in your future development. All of the college's student organizations welcome you aboard!

## **Student Union**

The **GC Student Union** is a vibrant and creative student organization dedicated to enriching campus life and building a high-quality platform for student growth and development. The Student Union comprises six departments — **Career Development Department**, **Internal Liaison Department**, **External Relations Department**, **Arts and Entertainment Department**, **Technical Department**, and **Sports and Club Department** — each with its own distinct character yet closely interwoven to support students' academic progress, career planning, and extracurricular activities.

### **Career Development Department**

The **Career Development Department** focuses on students' career growth and skill development. It operates through three functional teams — **Publicity Team**, **Events Team**, and **Career Planning Team** — covering event planning, organization, and promotion across areas like **career planning**, **experience sharing**, **job-seeking guidance**, and **skill development**.

- **Publicity Team** systematically delivers career development information and event updates by running the official WeChat public account **JIcareer**, publishing the *Set Off from JI* handbook, and producing themed posts
- **Events Team** serves as the external liaison hub, responsible for connecting with companies, alumni, and other student organizations to plan and execute company visits, alumni interviews, and other hands-on exchange activities
- **Career Planning Team** is dedicated to building an interactive platform linking GC students with outstanding alumni and industry leaders, offering personalized development guidance through collaborative events such as "Conversations with JI People"

Through team-based collaboration and integrated resources, the **Career Development Department** empowers students to grow holistically along their academic, professional, and personal paths. Here, you'll not only develop a wide range of skills but also gain fast-track access to company visits, diverse mentorship from alumni, and much more. Whether you have a clear vision for your career or are still exploring your direction, whether you excel at event planning or thrive on creative publicity, the Career Development Department welcomes you!

### **Internal Liaison Department**

The **Internal Liaison Department** serves as the communication hub within the Student Union, responsible for channeling information between the college administration and the student body, fostering internal unity within the Student Union, and organizing student activities and academic initiatives.

Upward, the department conveys students' concerns and requests to the relevant college offices, advocating for student rights and interests. Downward, it relays the college's decisions and responses back to every student.

Additionally, the department strives to bridge gaps between communities by organizing a variety of **cross-year** and **cross-cultural** exchange activities, creating opportunities to connect with students from different years and countries as well as professors. If you have a passion for student advocacy, want to build bridges of communication within the college, or wish to contribute to making the college a better place, the Internal Liaison Department is your **perfect choice**!

### **External Relations Department**

The **External Relations Department** is a vibrant and creative bridge within the GC Student Union. It is dedicated to designing and executing a rich array of high-quality events that closely connect GC students and foster a warm, friendly community atmosphere.

- As the internal liaison hub within the Student Union, the department ensures smooth cross-department and cross-organization collaboration
- It actively builds and maintains connections with outstanding student organizations at universities across Shanghai and beyond, organizing intercollegiate events and socials to promote exchange
- It hosts the much-loved **Boys' Day** and **Girls' Day** events, spreading warmth and celebration across campus
- It runs the upgraded "**Make Friends This Summer**" program, creating relaxed and fun opportunities for students to get to know one another

The department operates on a flexible, project-based model rather than fixed functional teams. Every member is encouraged to choose which event planning group to join based on their interests and strengths, freely taking on or sharing diverse roles. This approach maximizes initiative and creativity, allowing everyone to shine in the activities they're passionate about and build a well-rounded skill set.

### **Arts and Entertainment Department**

The **Arts and Entertainment Department** is the comprehensive department within the Student Union that organizes medium-to-large-scale cultural and artistic events, including the **Fall Welcome Party**, the **Spring Ball**, the **Summer GC Top 10 Singers Competition**, and more. As the department with the largest-scale events and the broadest reach, it is committed to creating fun, diverse, and international student activities.

The department is organized into:

- **Publicity Team** — produces posters, promotional posts, music videos, and other宣传 materials
- **Program Team** — conceptualizes performances, creates visual content, and orchestrates the event flow
- **Logistics Team** — handles venue booking, budget management, and on-site setup

The Arts and Entertainment Department maintains a clear tradition of experience-sharing and well-defined task planning, striving to deliver **the most creative experiences** for all participants while giving its members **maximum freedom and guidance**. The department is driven by young talent, with **first-year** students taking on directing roles for all events. Whether you dream of the stage and want to bring events to life with your creative vision, have experience in content production or large-scale event coordination, are passionate about visual design and eager to capture stunning moments through writing and photography, or are a detail-oriented executor who can flawlessly bring plans to life — the Arts and Entertainment Department **welcomes you**! With the department's well-documented **experience archives** and **workshops on promotional skills**, you can turn every creative idea into a spectacular event and gain invaluable organizational experience along the way.

### **Technical Department**

The **Technical Department** is a **hands-on technical platform** where students can put their skills to the test, dedicated to helping everyone level up in engineering and information technology. Never touched tech before? **No worries!** Here, you can learn the theory and build hard skills through real projects. The department is built around **flexible division of labor**, with functional modules including **Workshops**, **Technical Maintenance**, and **Project Development** — members can choose their path based on **interest**.

- In **Workshops**, we regularly host a variety of hands-on sessions on topics like **Linux Install Party**, **Git**, **Bash**, **Typst**, **reflow soldering**, and **mechanical keyboards**, supporting students in their learning journey. It's not just a place to learn — it's also a space to connect and explore the joy of technology with like-minded peers.

- The **Technical Maintenance Team** is responsible for keeping existing projects running and up to date, ensuring tools and services continue to serve students effectively. Along the way, you'll build solid practical experience and sharpen your problem-solving skills.

- With the **Project Development Team**, you'll have the chance to dive into new, creative builds. Past projects include the **VSCode JOJ Plugin**, the **Ball Dance Mini-Program**, and more. Current projects in development include a **Course Selection Community**, a **PDF Translation Tool**, with future plans for an **Event Calendar** and a **Canvas Desktop App**. This is the ultimate stage for turning ideas into reality and transforming skills into tangible impact.

- **Publicity Team** is primarily responsible for the Technical Department's posts on the **GC Blue Tiger** WeChat public account. Team members plan content, design layouts, and write copy, sharing the department's technical achievements and workshop highlights with a wider audience. It's a great way to hone your communication and design skills while gaining experience in teamwork and outreach.

Whether you want to build your technical chops through code, hardware, or project work, or you're interested in running a public account and trying your hand at content creation and design, you'll find your place in the **Technical Department**. There are diverse paths to choose from, real opportunities to practice, and teammates who grow right alongside you.

### **Sports and Club Department**

The **Sports and Club Department** is responsible for organizing and promoting **sports activities**, with three functional teams — **Logistics Team**, **Publicity Team**, and **Planning Team** — dedicated to offering students a wide range of sports events and experiences.
- **Logistics Team** prepares supplies for various activities, ensuring events run smoothly
- **Publicity Team** manages the public account, publishes posts, and recruits volunteers and participants to maximize event reach
- **Planning Team** focuses on designing event content and rules, creating sports competitions that balance fun and competitiveness

The department runs signature events throughout the year, including the **Glow Run** in the fall semester, the **Fun Sports Meet** in the spring, and the **Miyuan Cup** in the summer. The Sports and Club Department looks forward to welcoming students who are passionate about organizing sports activities. Whether you excel at planning, promotion, or design, or simply love sports competitions, there's a place for you here. Join the **Sports and Club Department** — ignite your **passion for sports** and help create **thrilling events**!

## **Science and Technology Innovation Association**

The **Student Science and Technology Innovation Association** (abbreviated as **S&T Association**) is a student organization dedicated to providing a high-quality **platform for technological innovation**. It consists of three departments: **Project Department**, **Competition Department**, and **Publicity Department**. Whether you're already a seasoned innovator or a newcomer eager to explore the world of tech, as long as you have a passion for creation, the GC S&T Association is your best choice.

### **Project Department**

The Project Department's main goal is to cultivate students' innovation and organizational skills. It is primarily responsible for **organizing the Freshman Mechanical Design Competition** and the **Meet Your Research Mentor** events. The Freshman Mechanical Design Competition is a campus-wide event where students build a remote-controlled vehicle capable of carrying objects and performing tasks from scratch, guided by the organizing team. This major event is entirely run by the S&T Association, with the Project Department taking the lead — from early corporate communications to mid-event venue setup and refereeing, to post-event evaluations and reimbursements. Meet Your Research Mentor, meanwhile, offers students a valuable opportunity to step outside the classroom, enter the lab, and connect directly with faculty researchers.

### **Competition Department**

The Competition Department is primarily responsible for **leading and organizing technical workshops** and **promoting and coordinating science and technology competitions**. During each year's competition registration periods, the department runs targeted preparation workshops. Beyond that, it regularly hosts in-depth lectures on topics such as technological innovation, mathematical modeling, mechanical design competitions, and major introductions. The Competition Department also gathers information on various science and technology competitions, promotes relevant opportunities to students, and organizes participation.

### **Publicity Department**

The Publicity Department's main role is managing the **GCInnovation+** WeChat public account, including competition announcements, lecture promotions, and post-event recaps. As the bridge connecting students with the S&T Association, the department handles科普宣传 and information dissemination. The public account regularly posts updates on competitions available to GC students, details about S&T Association events, and lecture introductions, helping GC students stay informed and get involved.

## **Advising Center**

The **Advising Center** (abbreviated as **AC**) is a dedicated platform providing comprehensive **academic support** for students. Over the past few years, AC has organized a series of content-rich, practical workshops featuring excellent senior students from various majors who share their experiences on key academic topics such as **academic planning**, **course selection guidance**, **program applications**, and **research internships**. In addition to regular workshops, AC's team of student assistants offers **both online and in-person advising services**, ready to answer any academic questions students may have.

To make learning resources easily accessible, AC has set up a Piazza Q&A platform and has archived all workshop materials in its official GitHub repository: [github.com/GC-Advising-Center](https://github.com/GC-Advising-Center/Workshops) and Workshop archive site: [ac.gcers.org](https://ac.gcers.org). Students can also access past materials through the "Resources" section on the AC WeChat public account.

## **Psychology Association**

The Psychology Association (GC Student Psychology Association) is SJTU's first student-led organization focused on mental health. It was initially formed around GC's class psychology representatives, later welcoming many more students interested in psychology and related fields. Over the past year, the association has organized **interdisciplinary academic lectures** on psychology and related topics, run **small-group in-depth counseling workshops**, and hosted **casual recreational events** such as **board game nights** at LBL, **piano concerts**, and the **Electric Meadow Music Festival** co-hosted at the electronic field. These activities have helped students better understand psychology in fun settings and find moments of peace away from their intense academic schedules.

The association's organizational structure is led by a presidium, with four departments: **Organization Department**, **Liaison Department**, **Publicity Department**, and **Internal Affairs Department**. The Organization Department handles activity design, the Liaison Department connects with on- and off-campus resources, the Publicity Department manages post and poster design, and the Internal Affairs Department secures venues and supplies.

The Psychology Association is a warm and lively family where everyone contributes their part, and it aims to continue creating a more comprehensive haven for the minds of students and faculty alike.

## **JI Story Interview Group**

The **JI Story Interview Group** is a warm-hearted campus media outlet and a cultural oasis steeped in sentiment. Affiliated with the college's Youth League Committee, it was founded on the 10th anniversary of GC and has since been dedicated to getting to know everyone connected to GC, capturing the unique spirit of the GC community, and chronicling the college's journey. Its work spans **personal interviews**, **historical documentation**, and **cultural storytelling**. The group's carefully curated WeChat public account, "**JI Stories Decade**," has published over 60 **in-depth personal interviews** and built a loyal readership of over 3,000. "JI Stories" refers to everyone who shares a connection with GC — alumni, students, faculty, and leadership alike. Through vivid storytelling, the group ensures that GC's unique cultural character is passed down through generations.

The interview process begins with the team precisely planning the topic, selecting the interviewee, reaching out, and crafting the interview outline. The core interview is then conducted in depth, with key information carefully captured and refined through multiple rounds of drafting and polish to produce high-quality articles. Finally, the stories are published across WeChat public accounts and video channels, with simultaneous distribution to college and university official channels for wider reach.

The JI Story Interview Group's current work is vibrant and diverse. Last summer, responding to the university Youth League Committee's call, it launched the "JI Whispers" social practice project, using interviews to preserve valuable footnotes to GC's 20-year history. It innovatively introduced the "Voice of JI" podcast program, using audio to bring GC's interesting characters and cultural stories to life. It continues to deepen its flagship "Conversations with JI People" series, featuring in-depth dialogues with alumni, professors, and university leadership — not only presenting a richer, more multifaceted image of faculty but also addressing common questions on students' minds. Looking ahead, the group is actively exploring more innovative, in-depth, and meaningful interview directions, such as focusing on international students' academic journeys, future plans, and integration into the college, steadily expanding its horizons toward a more global stage.
